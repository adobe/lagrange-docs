template<typename Scalar>
struct lagrange::raycasting::detail::FacetTriangle< Scalar >

Precomputed world-space facet triangle for O(1) ray origins: base = V2, edge0 = V0 - V2, edge1 = V1 - V2, so a barycentric (b0, b1) maps to base + b0*edge0 + b1*edge1.
