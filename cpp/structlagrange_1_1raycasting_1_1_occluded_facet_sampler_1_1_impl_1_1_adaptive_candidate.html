Detailed Description

template<typename Scalar, typename Index>
struct lagrange::raycasting::OccludedFacetSampler< Scalar, Index >::Impl::AdaptiveCandidate

Seed for an Adaptive ray cast: a visible neighbor and its cached escape direction.

Only the direction is borrowed — rays still originate on the facet under test so the K confirmations actually attest to its visibility.
